rclone — personal backup client

This is a private, single-user Google OAuth client. It lets rclone, running on its owner's own research server, copy that same person's research files into, and back out of, the owner's own Google Drive.

  • One user: the account owner, who is also the developer of this client.
  • Not distributed, not offered as a service, no other users.
  • Access is limited to files this client itself created (drive.file).
  • No analytics, no telemetry, no third-party services.
