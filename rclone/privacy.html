Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

This OAuth client is used solely by its owner, for rclone backups of the owner's own files to the owner's own Google Drive. The person who created it is the only person who uses it.

  • No data is collected, stored or shared with any third party.
  • No analytics, tracking or telemetry of any kind.
  • OAuth credentials are stored only on the owner's own machine and are sent to no one but Google.
  • Data moves only between the owner's own machine and the owner's own Google Drive; the client can access only files it created itself (drive.file).
  • No data is sold or used for advertising or profiling.

Contact: the owner of the GitHub account hosting this page, via that account.
